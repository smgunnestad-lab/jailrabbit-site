Rules

Friendly competition, serious bragging rights.

  1. 01

    The challenge runs from 3 August through 1 September 2026.

  2. 02

    You must use your main or highest-ranked account. Accounts will be checked.

  3. 03

    You must already have played at least 10 ranked games this season. Emerald and Diamond players need at least 25.

  4. 04

    Duo play with other competitors is allowed. Intentional losing is not.

  5. 05

    Boosting results in immediate disqualification for everyone involved.

  6. 06

    You may enter both games, but each game has its own leaderboard and winner.

  7. 07

    Your account must be submitted within 48 hours and approved before tracking begins.

  8. 08

    Accounts cannot be swapped, and new competitors cannot join after registration closes.

  9. 09

    Toxicity and overly negative behaviour are not tolerated.
